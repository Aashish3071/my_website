AuraDrive is published by [company name] ("we"). This policy explains what the AuraDrive app does
with information.

## What the app collects

Nothing. AuraDrive has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. It does not send any
information about you or your diffuser to us or to anyone else.

## What is stored on your iPhone

The names and settings of the diffusers you add, and your choices in Settings. This stays on your
iPhone and is removed when you delete the app.

## Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used only to find and control your diffuser. It is not used to work out your location.

## Notifications

If you turn on low fragrance alerts, the app shows a notification on your iPhone when a cartridge
is running low. Notifications are created on your iPhone and are not sent through our servers.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If the app starts collecting any information in a later version, this policy will be updated
before that version is released.
