## Description (4,000 max)

AuraDrive is the companion app for your [brand] car fragrance diffuser.

Control your diffuser
• Turn it on and off from your iPhone
• Choose from five intensity levels
• Run in intervals so the fragrance lasts longer
• Set a sleep timer to turn it off automatically

Know when to replace the cartridge
• See how much fragrance is left
• Get an alert when a cartridge is running low

Made for everyone
• Works with VoiceOver, Larger Text, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion
• Light and dark appearance

Private by design
• No account needed
• No advertising and no tracking

Requires a [brand, model] diffuser.
